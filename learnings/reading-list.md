# Reading list

Free sources worth mining for this folder. Mark each one when it has been
read and its useful parts written up.

| Source | Covers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics – A Design Guide](https://cdn.sparkfun.com/assets/home_page_posts/1/4/1/0/Plastic_Snap_fit_design.pdf) (Bayer MaterialScience) | Cantilever, torsion and annular snaps: strain limits, forces | Done: [snap-fits.md](snap-fits.md) |
| [A Guide to Additive Manufacturing](https://link.springer.com/978-3-031-05863-9) (Springer 2022, open access) | Design for AM in general; anisotropy | To read |
| [Basics of 3D Printing with Josef Prusa](https://www.prusa3d.com/product/basics-of-3d-printing-with-josef-prusa) (free e-book) | Slicer settings, overhangs, bridging, materials | To read |
| [BYU Compliant Mechanisms Research Group](https://compliantmechanisms.byu.edu) | A library of flexures and compliant mechanisms | To browse |
| CNC Kitchen (Stefan Hermann), videos and blog | Measured layer adhesion, infill, walls, PLA creep | To read |
| Bambu Lab wiki | P1S tolerances and filament data | To read |

Paid, and worth buying if the free ones run out: *A Practical Guide to
Design for Additive Manufacturing* (Diegel, Springer 2019) and *Handbook of
Compliant Mechanisms* (Howell, Magleby, Olsen; Wiley).
