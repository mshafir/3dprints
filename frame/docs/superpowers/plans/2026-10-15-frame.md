# Lattice picture frame — implementation plan

**Goal:** A parametric CadQuery script that generates print-ready STEP and STL
files for a lattice picture frame for 11 x 8.5 in contents, cut into as few
sections as fit the bed, with automated fit checks and a Bambu Studio project.

**Architecture:** One module, `frame.py`, holding a frozen `Params` dataclass,
size helpers (rabbet, sight, outer, band, section count), shared solid
builders, feature builders that work on the top side and are turned onto the
other three (lattice runs, keyholes, button pockets and pegs), `make_body()` for
the whole frame, `make_sections()` to cut it at the seams with dovetails,
`fit_test_parts()` cropped from the real sections, and `main()` to export.
The frame is modelled face down in print orientation, centred on the origin.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12 via uv, CadQuery 2.8, pytest.

**Spec:** `frame/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-frame-design.md`

## Global constraints

- PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, no supports: no downward face flatter
  than 45 degrees.
- Every part within 250 x 250 mm.
- Bars and walls at least 1.6 mm.
- No hardware or glue.
